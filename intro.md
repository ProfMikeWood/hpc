# High Performance Computing

Welcome to this resource on high performance computing clusters!

In my position at the Moss Landing Marine Labs and in the Department of Computer Science at San José State University (SJSU), I use high performance computing clusters (or "supercomputers") often in my research and teaching. As a result, I am regularly asked questions about how to use, navigate, and access these resources. Typically, these questions come from colleagues and students at SJSU who are interested in using the Spartan computing cluster supported by SJSU's College of Science. Since I often convey this information in one-on-one meetings, I decided to collect this information in one concise place with the hope that it can be widely useful as a reference at our university and beyond. 

In the pages linked below, you'll find lots of information on how to access and use Spartan in particular. However, these instructions are common to many high performance computing clusters and will likely be useful to researchers at other institutions - I may add some notes on other clusters in the future. 

If you find any errors, typos, or anything that is unclear in these pages, please let me know. I make a lot of mistakes and sometimes skip over important details. I am happy to update these pages with any feedback you may provide. You can email me directly (mike.wood@sjsu.edu) or you can submit an issue on the Github repository for this book (https://github.com/ProfMikeWood/hpc).
